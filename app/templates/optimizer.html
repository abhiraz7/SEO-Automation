AI Content Optimizer
Pick one of your pages and a keyword. The tool compares the page with the pages that rank for it and proposes up to 5 small, specific edits, each backed by evidence and checked automatically before you see it as ready. It can also say no change recommended. Every edit is a draft: you accept, edit or reject it, and nothing is published automatically. Each run makes one search-results call (skipped if a recent analysis of this page and keyword exists) and one AI call.
